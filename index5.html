Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding general treatments, security, and onboarding practices.

How often should I schedule a dental checkup?

We universally recommend scheduling comprehensive dynamic scaling diagnostic assessments at least once every six months to guarantee maximum early detection protection.

Are your teeth whitening treatments safe for sensitive teeth?

Yes, completely. Our premium in-office whitening structures utilize precise, desensitizing laser configurations and protective sealants engineered to eliminate deep enamel discoloration with zero microscopic nerve pain or enamel wear.

Do you accept insurance or offer financing options?

SmileCare partners smoothly with most leading premium provider networks. We also supply interest-free monthly installment packages to fit advanced cosmetic aesthetic treatments nicely into your budget plans.
